What Story of My League tracks

Paste your Sleeper League ID above and get a full history of your fantasy football league — no setup, no account, nothing to connect. It's free and works with any Sleeper league.

  • Historical standings and head-to-head records across every season
  • A championships-weighted GOAT ranking — not just win/loss
  • Automatically generated storylines: your league's most lopsided rivalry, best draft steal, worst bench call, and biggest trade blowout
  • A Luck Index comparing your actual record to an all-play expected record
  • Trophy Case, Playoff Bracket, Power Rankings, and Season Trend for every resolved season
  • Shareable stat cards you can post straight to your league group chat
